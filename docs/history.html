Before streaming · the iTunes library, 2013–14

Two library snapshots, September 2013 and April 2014 — cumulative play counts from the pre-streaming years, a different kind of record than the river above.

About this data. 2018–2026 come from Spotify’s GDPR extended streaming history (a play = ≥30 seconds listened). The 2013–14 shelf comes from two iTunes library XML snapshots with cumulative play counts. In between is thin ground: 2015–16 saw only a handful of streams and 2017 is absent from the export entirely, so those years are reported, not drawn. Household plays (kids’ shows, lullaby white noise, the Frozen soundtrack) are tagged and hidden by default — flip the toggle to see the full house. Part of the Discovery showcase.
